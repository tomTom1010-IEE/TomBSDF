## Energy And Transport Scope

- Thirty coefficient cases compare the closed incoherent slab budget with an
  independent 1024-term internal-return sum. Worst error is 5.5512e-16; all
  reflection/transmission coefficients are nonnegative and their sum is <=1.
- Nine white-environment GPU cases cover IOR 1/1.5/2.4 and roughness 0/0.4/0.8.
  Worst center RGB error is 5.9605e-8. These validate Probe/DFG plus slab-budget
  composition; they are **not** an independent angular integral of a new exact
  microfacet BTDF. The existing compensated reflection core is unchanged.
- RGB absorption remains chromatic without the companion; a scalar alpha blend
  cannot substitute for the measured red/green/blue transmission.
- Thickness-map multiplication, zero coverage, fractional coverage and IOR=1
  neutral behavior pass. Shader-owned energy resources are retained by the
  new carrier's dependencies and bound through ShaderImporter APIs.
- Native Base/Add reflection, Probe, anisotropy, detail normal/AA and both area
  qualities contribute. The aligned directional-shadow positive control changes
  center direct radiance by 2.9543; the optical transmission budget is unchanged.
- Existing opaque and ordinary coverage Alpha service-on/off controls match
  exactly, as do all 39 pre-glass shader/include hashes.

## Delivery And Limits

`m_BSDFGlass` / `a_BSDFGlass` and the three accepted shader-owned resources were
bound via `preparebsdfglass`, not handwritten Unity files. Manifest registration,
all 55 public controls/textures and their tooltips are generated from the glass
contract and selected common inputs. Companion 0.5.0 builds without warnings or
errors; its authoring runtime DLL matches that build.

This is selected compilation and Editor rendering, not exhaustive distribution
compilation, player ME/save-import acceptance or a GPU frame-time certification.
The distribution bundle remains unchanged at SHA256
`9EC10C0BF0C4A0563513CED444FBA140D26DBC2DD9859C01A14283B42B138C94`.
No package/plugin was deployed to the game, no KK adaptation was done.

The model is a local parallel slab with supplied thickness, not true closed-mesh
lens tracing, camera-inside-medium tracking, exact rough BTDF or nested volumes.
The scene snapshot contains opaque/cutout/sky content, not other transparent
layers or hidden/offscreen surfaces. Rough transmission is a positive nine-tap
approximation. There are no caustics, colored/physical glass shadow casting,
extra shadow-map generation, SSR, automatic thickness, or coat/film-on-glass
controls. See [Glass-Contract.md](Glass-Contract.md) for the exact fallback formula.
